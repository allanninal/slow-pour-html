Why we pour every Oat Flat White by hand

Regulars know the tulip on top of an Oat Flat White, and plenty of them photograph it. For us it is a quick quality check: the pattern only holds when the milk is steamed properly.

Oat milk takes less heat and less air than dairy. Too much of either and it separates in the cup. So we steam it until the jug is just warm to hold, then pour from close to the surface so the crema stays sweet.

Want the same coffee at home? Our House Blend subscription sends a freshly roasted 250g bag every month, and it is made for milk drinks. You can cancel anytime.
