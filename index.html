Direct Trade

Our beans, their story

We don't buy commodities. We buy relationships. Every bag we sell traces back to a specific farmer, a specific harvest, a specific hillside. We visit both farms every year — and we pay a meaningful premium above the going market price because quality coffee begins with quality partnerships.

🇪🇹 Ethiopia

Yirgacheffe

Dara Hills Cooperative

Light Roast
Farmer
Meseret Tafari
Process
Natural
Tasting Notes
Stone fruit · Jasmine · Bergamot

The Dara Hills Cooperative sits at 1,900–2,200 m in the Gedeo highlands. Meseret has been farming these heirloom varietals for over two decades. Natural-process drying on raised beds gives our filter roast its distinctive blueberry-jam sweetness and violet aromatics.

🇨🇴 Colombia

Huila

Finca El Alba Verde

Medium Roast
Farmer
Mateo Rincón
Process
Washed
Tasting Notes
Dark chocolate · Blood orange · Walnut

Mateo Rincón and his family tend Caturra and Castillo varietals at 1,750 m above the Magdalena River valley. Fully washed and dried on mechanical driers, this lot forms the backbone of our espresso blend — clean, structured, with a long finish.

Our Story

Born from a road trip to Yirgacheffe.

Amara and Kwesi Osei grew up in a Ghanaian-American household in southeast Portland where the coffee was always from a supermarket can and always too strong. When Amara took a fellowship to Addis Ababa in 2016, she wandered into a coffee ceremony in a family home in Yirgacheffe and drank something she describes as "the opposite of what I thought coffee was."

She called Kwesi that night. Two years later, they had visited both Ethiopia and Colombia, built relationships with Meseret Tafari at the Dara Hills Cooperative and Mateo Rincón at Finca El Alba Verde, and signed a lease on a 900 sq ft space on NW 23rd.

They named the shop Mansa — "king" or "chief" in Manding — not out of grandeur, but out of conviction: good coffee deserves to be treated like royalty, from seed to cup.

Goldie, the copper lever espresso machine behind the bar, was their first major purchase. She's been running six days a week (every day but Monday) since opening day in March 2019.

"We want you to know exactly where your coffee comes from and whose hands picked it. That transparency doesn't cost extra — it's just honesty."

— Amara Osei, Co-founder
